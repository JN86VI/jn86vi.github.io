A full Android phone app is planned for normal incoming, outgoing and active calls.

Planned

Phone essentials

Dialpad and T9 search, Android system contacts and call history, Favorites, Dual SIM, block list, Speed Dial and call notes are in the planned V1. Core calling remains available in Free.

Configurable Redial

Redial is part of Dialer. The earlier Redial proof of concept is a technical predecessor, not a separate JN86 app.

One visual base

V1 uses one JN86 accent and System, Light and Dark appearance modes. A multi-theme or custom color picker is outside V1.

Free and Premium

The app is planned without ads or subscriptions. A one-time lifetime Premium purchase adds more Redial attempts, unlimited call notes and selected customization. The phone's essential functions remain free.

Dialer is planned; it has not been released on Google Play. Its dedicated privacy policy will need approval before publication.
