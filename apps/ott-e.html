Ott-e!

An ad-free interactive hunting game designed specifically for cats.

In DevelopmentAndroid

The idea

The screen belongs to the cat

Ott-e! is not a traditional human-controlled game.

No score. No profile. No clutter.

There is no leaderboard, registration or online game state.

Prey

Seven different hunting targets

Each prey has its own movement character and hit-point behavior.

Fly · Mosquito · Ladybird · Beetle · Cockroach · Grasshopper · Spider

Smaller prey use 1 HP, while tougher prey use 2 HP.

Movement & senses

Unpredictable, but not chaotic

Prey movement is continuous and controlled rather than chaotic.

Sound and haptics

Species-specific local sounds and optional haptics support the hunt.

Owner controls

Hidden from accidental paws

Owner controls stay hidden during normal play.

Tablet first, phone too

The play area adapts to portrait and landscape layouts.

Privacy & offline

No account. No server required.

The core game works fully offline using local resources.

Ad-free by design

Ott-e! is planned as a completely ad-free game.

No public release date yet

Fast. Simple. Reliable.
