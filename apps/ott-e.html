Ott-e!

A simple touch game designed primarily for cats, with moving insects and owner-controlled settings.

A playful screen for curious paws

Ott-e! is designed as a lightweight cat game where moving prey appears on screen and reacts to touch.

Planned highlights

  • Seven prey types with varied movement and behaviour
  • Touch reactions, hit states and simple visual feedback
  • Optional sound and vibration controlled by the owner
  • Owner menu for timing and gameplay preferences
  • Designed for tablets first, while remaining usable on phones

Designed for offline play

Core gameplay is intended to use local resources and work without an account, server connection or online profile.

Release status

Ott-e! is currently in development. No public release date has been announced yet.
