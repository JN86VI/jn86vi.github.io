About JN86

Fast. Simple. Reliable.

JN86 is a family of Android apps built around shared principles: fast, simple and reliable.

Familiar from the first use

JN86 apps share common visual and interaction foundations. If you have used one JN86 app, another should feel familiar in the way it looks and works.

Less unnecessary complexity

We do not add features just to make a feature list longer. A new feature should provide real value. If it does not make an app better, simpler or more reliable, it may not need to be there.

Privacy from the start

Privacy by Design and data minimization are part of how JN86 apps are developed. We use only data and permissions that are genuinely needed for an app to work, and we avoid unnecessary data collection and storage.

Where the purpose of an app allows it, we prefer simple local operation and avoid making core functions depend on unnecessary external services.

About the developer

JN86 is an independent, one-person development project. The apps are designed and developed in the developer’s free time alongside a full-time job.

The project is growing gradually: the goal is not to produce apps as quickly as possible, but to create apps with a clear purpose that are easy to use and remain reliable tools over the long term.

One app family, shared principles

JN86 does not mean that every app is the same. It means that the same core principles guide them.
