Frequently asked questions

Who is Ott-e! for?

Ott-e! is designed primarily for cats. The play screen focuses on moving prey instead of human game controls.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Core gameplay is designed to work fully offline using local images, animations and sounds.

Can the owner change settings?

Yes. Planned owner controls include session length, allowed prey, sound, haptics and display behavior.

Does the game contain ads?

Ott-e! is planned as an ad-free game. Optional support for JN86 is separate from gameplay and does not unlock features.

Is the app already on Google Play?

Not yet. Ott-e! is still in development and no public release date has been announced.
