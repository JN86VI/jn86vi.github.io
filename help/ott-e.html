Help for the Ott e! Android game.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Ott e! for?

Ott e! is designed primarily for cats. The play screen focuses on moving prey instead of human game controls.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Gameplay works fully offline using local images, animations and sounds.

Can the owner change settings?

Yes. The owner can choose the session length and prey, and turn sound and vibration on or off.

Does the game contain ads?

Ott e! is ad-free. Optional support for JN86 is separate from gameplay and does not unlock features.

Is the app already on Google Play?

Ott e! is publicly available on Google Play.
