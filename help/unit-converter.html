Pre-release help for the Unit Converter Android app.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Conversions and calculations that do not require external data are designed to work fully offline.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the core conversion features.

What can it convert?

The planned first release covers everyday, engineering, electrical, fluid and other technical categories.

Can I save frequently used tools?

Favorites, recent items, search and configurable number formatting are planned for fast access.

Is the app already on Google Play?

Not yet. Unit Converter is still in development and no public release date has been announced.
