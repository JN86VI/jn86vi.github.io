Ott e! Privacy Policy

For the Ott e! Android app (hu.jn86.otte). Developer: JN86VI.

App data and local settings

Ott e! is free and ad-free. Gameplay works offline without a JN86 account or server. The app has no Ads, Analytics, Premium, subscriptions or in-app purchases, and does not send gameplay data to JN86. It keeps your selected language, theme, session length, enabled prey, sound and vibration in an app-private preferences database. Gameplay sessions are not saved. Resetting settings clears those choices; clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local copy.

Android system backup

If Android backup is enabled, the preferences database can be included in Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer and later restored. No other app data is included by the app backup rules. JN86 does not run a separate cloud backup. The Android backup provider controls its copies; clearing local app data does not necessarily delete an earlier backup. Manage backups in your device and account settings.

External website, contact and support

Privacy and Support links open the JN86 website in a browser; Contact opens your email app. The site uses GitHub Pages, whose operator may process technical visit data. Optional Support development grants no digital content or advantage. A PayPal contribution is handled by PayPal, which may give the creator transaction and contact details. A Bitcoin transfer is visible on the public blockchain; a wallet address alone does not necessarily identify its sender to JN86. If you email us, your email address and message reach JN86VI. These external actions are initiated by you.

External privacy notices: GitHub · PayPal

Retention and deletion

Local settings remain until reset, app-data clearing or uninstall; Android backup copies follow the backup provider rules. We keep contact messages only while the inquiry and necessary follow-up remain open, then delete or anonymize them unless a legal obligation or claim requires longer. Transaction information received by the creator is retained only for applicable tax or accounting duties and legal claims, then deleted or anonymized where possible. PayPal applies its own retention rules; JN86 cannot erase public blockchain records. We process contact messages to respond to requests on the basis of legitimate interests; legally required transaction records are processed to meet legal obligations.

Security

Settings use Android app-private storage. The app does not transmit gameplay data to a JN86 server. Links to the JN86 website use HTTPS. Email, Android backup and payment providers apply their own security practices. Access to contact and transaction information directly received by JN86VI is limited to the creator and any parties needed for legal or accounting duties.

Your choices and requests

You can reset settings or clear app data on your device and manage Android backups through your device or account. There is no JN86 account to delete. To request access, correction or deletion of information held by JN86VI, email us. Legally required records may have to be retained; public blockchain entries cannot be removed by JN86VI. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and complain to your data protection authority.
