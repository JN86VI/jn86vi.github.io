Ott-e!

Privacy information

Pre-release privacy information for the Ott-e! Android app.

Offline by design

Ott-e! is designed so its core gameplay works fully offline. Images, animations and sounds used during play are local app resources.

Personal data

Core gameplay does not require a name, email address, account, contacts, location, camera or microphone. No online profile or leaderboard is required.

Local settings

Owner-selected settings such as session length, allowed prey, sound, haptics or display behavior may be stored locally on the device so the app can remember them.

Advertising and support

Ott-e! is planned as an ad-free app. Optional support for JN86 is separate from gameplay, is handled outside the app, and does not unlock features, content or advantages.

No JN86 server required

The planned core game does not require a JN86 backend or account service. Gameplay data is not intended to be uploaded to a JN86 server.

Before public release

Ott-e! is still in development. This page will be reviewed against the final released build before publication and updated if any data handling changes.
