Calculations on your device

Calculations work offline, without a JN86 account or calculation server. Inputs, expressions, results and explanations are processed on your device. The app does not send them, your calculation history or memory to JN86 or supply them to advertising or payment services. An unfinished input, selection and temporary display state may disappear when the app process ends.

History, memory and settings

The app saves up to 100 successful calculations in its private storage: inputs or expressions, operation, result, relevant parameters, language context, time, order and a calculation-record ID. New entries replace the oldest above that limit. A single exact memory value and inch rounding settings also persist after closing the app. Language and theme are stored separately. These records let you recall calculations and keep your preferences; record IDs are not JN86 user accounts.

Retention and local deletion

You can delete individual History entries or clear all History after confirmation. MC clears only memory. Clearing the calculator input does not delete History or memory; deleting History does not clear memory or the current input. Settings and memory remain until changed or cleared; History has no time-based expiry. Android’s Clear storage/app data or uninstall removes the app’s local data. Copies in backups, the clipboard or another app must be managed separately and can outlive local deletion.

Android backup and restore

Android may back up and restore saved History, memory, inch settings, language and theme through cloud backup or device transfer. Availability depends on Android, your device, account and backup settings; transfer is not guaranteed on every phone. Temporary editing data, caches, consent/session state and ad-free purchase entitlement are excluded from this backup. JN86 provides no separate cloud sync or manual backup-file export/import. Your backup provider controls transport and retention; manage earlier copies in device/account settings. Restoring a backup may bring back previously saved records.

Copy, paste and sharing

Copy or Cut places the selected text or result on the Android clipboard at your request; Paste inserts clipboard text into the editor. Share sends the selected successful calculation’s expression and result as plain text through Android’s app chooser. It does not automatically share the whole History or memory. The app you select may store or upload that text under its own policy. There is no direct calculation-file export or import.

Google consent in the release version

In the planned release, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) will check your consent status when the app enters the foreground, after you choose the app language. To decide whether to show a form, UMP can contact Google before you agree to ads. It can send your IP address, app and device details, screen information, language and stored consent signals. UMP stores your choices on the device. Google can also process your interactions with the form. Where available, use the app’s privacy options to review or withdraw consent.

Banner advertising in the release version

The release version will start Google Mobile Ads SDK Next-Gen 1.6.0 for AdMob only when the free version can request a banner in the foreground. The app requests personalized ads only if your consent settings allow personalization. Unknown or blocked permission and an active ad-free purchase each prevent requests. Declining ads does not automatically enable non-personalized or limited ads. When only non-personalized ads are permitted, the app requests them explicitly. These ads can still use identifiers for measurement or fraud prevention.

While the ads SDK is active, it automatically collects and shares data with Google and participating ad partners for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. This includes your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions such as launches and taps, performance and crash diagnostics such as startup time or reports of freezes and crashes, and identifiers. These can include the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device. The consent form lists potential partners. The app sends no calculation inputs, results, history or JN86 user ID to AdMob.

Optional one-time ad removal

The release version will offer a one-time Google Play Billing purchase to remove ads. All calculations will be free, with no subscription. Play will show the current regional price and handle payment. To verify, acknowledge or restore a purchase, the app uses the product identifier, purchase state, purchase token and purchase data signed by Google Play. It checks the signature on the device without sending the token to a JN86 server. JN86 receives no payment card or bank account details. Billing can send technical diagnostics to Google even if you never open the purchase screen. Buying and restoring are independent of ad consent.

A protected record on the device keeps the last verified ad-free entitlement available offline. Restore checks with Play. A pending purchase does not grant ad removal, and a confirmed refund or revocation removes the entitlement. The record is excluded from Android backup. Clearing app data does not erase the Play transaction or request a refund.

Permissions, diagnostics and security

Calculations require no camera, microphone, contacts or GPS permission. The planned Google integration may use network access, the advertising ID (AD_ID) and basic phone state such as mobile network type (READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE). App-owned diagnostics use local technical logs; there is no JN86 analytics or crash-upload service. Google SDK diagnostics are separate. Local records use Android app-private storage; the Google service connections and website links use HTTPS/TLS. This does not promise that every backup is end-to-end encrypted. Your device, keyboard, backup provider and recipient apps apply their own security practices.

Website, email and voluntary support

Website and Support links open your browser; Contact opens your email app. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process IP addresses and technical visit data. The site remembers your chosen language in browser storage. Emailing JN86VI shares your address and message with us and the email providers. We use them to answer your request, on the basis of legitimate interests, and delete or anonymize them after necessary follow-up unless legal obligations or claims require longer retention. Voluntary support gives no app benefit: PayPal may share transaction/contact details with the creator; Bitcoin transfers are public blockchain records. Any transaction records we receive are retained as required for accounting, tax or legal claims. JN86 cannot erase blockchain records.

Your choices and privacy contact

Use local deletion controls, Android backup and advertising-ID settings, and Google’s privacy controls for the relevant data. Buying ad removal stops banners, not necessarily consent checks, Billing or other external services. Google and other providers have their own retention and deletion rules; we cannot promise a single retention period or erase all their records. For access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights concerning data held by JN86VI, email the contact below. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and complain to your data protection authority. There is no JN86 account to delete.
