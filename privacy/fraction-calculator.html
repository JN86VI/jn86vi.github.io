Calculations on your device

Calculations work offline, without a JN86 account or calculation server. Inputs, expressions, results and explanations are processed on your device. The app does not send them, your calculation history or memory to JN86 or supply them to advertising or payment services. An unfinished input, selection and temporary display state may disappear when the app process ends.

History, memory and settings

The app saves up to 100 successful calculations in its private storage: inputs or expressions, operation, result, relevant parameters, language context, time, order and a calculation-record ID. New entries replace the oldest above that limit. A single exact memory value and inch rounding settings also persist after closing the app. Language and theme are stored separately. These records let you recall calculations and keep your preferences; record IDs are not JN86 user accounts.

Retention and local deletion

You can delete individual History entries or clear all History after confirmation. MC clears only memory. Clearing the calculator input does not delete History or memory; deleting History does not clear memory or the current input. Settings and memory remain until changed or cleared; History has no time-based expiry. Android’s Clear storage/app data or uninstall removes the app’s local data. Copies in backups, the clipboard or another app must be managed separately and can outlive local deletion.

Android backup and restore

Android may back up and restore saved History, memory, inch settings, language and theme through cloud backup or device transfer. Availability depends on Android, your device, account and backup settings; transfer is not guaranteed on every phone. Temporary editing data, caches, consent/session state and ad-free purchase entitlement are excluded from this backup. JN86 provides no separate cloud sync or manual backup-file export/import. Your backup provider controls transport and retention; manage earlier copies in device/account settings. Restoring a backup may bring back previously saved records.

Copy, paste and sharing

Copy or Cut places the selected text or result on the Android clipboard at your request; Paste inserts clipboard text into the editor. Share sends the selected successful calculation’s expression and result as plain text through Android’s app chooser. It does not automatically share the whole History or memory. The app you select may store or upload that text under its own policy. There is no direct calculation-file export or import.

Your advertising choices

After you choose the app language, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks your privacy status when the app is in the foreground and has an internet connection. To decide whether to show a form, UMP can contact Google before you agree to ads. It can send your IP address, app and device details, screen information, language and stored consent signals. UMP stores your choices on the device. Google can also process your interactions with the form.

Separate European and US privacy messages are configured in AdMob. UMP determines which message and choices apply; neither form is shown in every country. When UMP requires a privacy entry point, open Settings → Privacy options in the app to review or change your choices, including withdrawing consent or opting out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising where available. The form and available options depend on your region. Your choices do not restrict the app’s calculation functions.

AdMob banner ads

The free app starts Google’s Next-Gen Mobile Ads SDK for AdMob only when it may request a banner while online and in the foreground. Unresolved or blocked advertising permission prevents requests. The applicable region and your actual privacy choices determine how advertising is handled, as described below.

Europe: EU/EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

UMP and the European Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) signals determine whether personalized ads, non-personalized ads or no Google ads are permitted. Personalized ads require the relevant consent. Non-personalized ads are requested only when the necessary device-storage consent and other applicable purposes and provider conditions are met. If a refusal leaves these conditions unmet, the app sends no Google ad request; it does not automatically switch to non-personalized or limited ads (LTD). Non-personalized ads may still use identifiers for frequency capping, measurement or fraud prevention.

Applicable US states

UMP records the applicable US privacy choices using Global Privacy Platform (GPP) signals. A supported opt-out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising causes Google to apply restricted data processing (RDP) to permitted ad requests. Google may still serve contextual, non-personalized ads. Opting out does not automatically remove advertising or grant Premium. An unresolved, invalid or unsupported applicable privacy signal prevents ad requests.

The AdMob US partner setting is “Only Google” for traffic from applicable US states. It covers requests both with and without RDP; these banner placements have no separately added US mediation sources. This setting is regional: it does not mean that only Google can supply ads worldwide. Elsewhere, participating ad partners depend on the applicable regional rules and settings.

Other countries

Outside the regions where European TCF or US GPP signals apply, the app does not automatically require a European consent signal. Ad requests depend on UMP’s current permission; Google’s SDK handles the applicable privacy signals and local requirements. The absence of a European or US form does not mean that local privacy rules do not apply, and is not treated by the app as consent to personalized advertising.

Depending on the region, your privacy choices and the ad mode, the ads SDK may collect and share data with Google and participating ad partners for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. This includes your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions such as launches and taps, performance and crash diagnostics such as startup time or reports of freezes and crashes, and identifiers. These can include the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device. Where a partner list is shown, it identifies potential providers, not the recipients of every request. RDP restricts the use of certain identifiers and data. The app sends no calculation inputs, results, history or JN86 user ID to AdMob.

The free app can show a built-in link to the JN86 website while no AdMob banner is available, including offline. Displaying this local promotion does not itself send an ad request. Opening the website in your browser requires an internet connection.

With verified Premium ad removal, the app shows no Google banner, local JN86 promotion or empty advertising strip, including offline. Premium does not necessarily stop UMP privacy checks, Play Billing or external services that you open yourself. Buying or restoring Premium is independent of your advertising choices.

Optional one-time ad removal

Google Play Billing offers a one-time purchase to remove ads, with no subscription. All calculations remain free. Play shows the current regional price and handles payment; buying and restoring require an internet connection. To verify, acknowledge or restore a purchase, the app uses the product identifier, purchase state, purchase token and purchase data signed by Google Play. It checks the signature on the device without sending the token to a JN86 server. JN86 receives no payment card or bank account details. Billing can send technical diagnostics to Google even if you never open the purchase screen.

A protected record on the device keeps the last verified ad-free entitlement available offline. Restore checks current ownership with Google Play. A pending purchase does not grant ad removal. If Play confirms that the purchase is no longer owned, for example after revocation, the app removes the entitlement. Temporary connection problems do not erase the last verified entitlement. The local record is excluded from Android backup. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not erase the Play transaction or request a refund.

Permissions, diagnostics and security

Calculations require no camera, microphone, contacts or GPS permission. The Google integration can use network access, the advertising ID (AD_ID) and basic phone state such as mobile network type (READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE). App-owned diagnostics use local technical logs; there is no JN86 analytics or crash-upload service. Google SDK diagnostics are separate. Local records use Android app-private storage; the Google service connections and website links use HTTPS/TLS. This does not promise that every backup is end-to-end encrypted. Your device, keyboard, backup provider and recipient apps apply their own security practices.

Website, email and voluntary support

Website and Support links open your browser; Contact opens your email app. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process IP addresses and technical visit data. The site remembers your chosen language in browser storage. Emailing JN86VI shares your address and message with us and the email providers. We use them to answer your request, on the basis of legitimate interests, and delete or anonymize them after necessary follow-up unless legal obligations or claims require longer retention. Voluntary support gives no app benefit: PayPal may share transaction/contact details with the creator; Bitcoin transfers are public blockchain records. Any transaction records we receive are retained as required for accounting, tax or legal claims. JN86 cannot erase blockchain records.
