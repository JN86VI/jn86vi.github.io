Inventory / ReadyStock

Planned privacy information

This page describes planned data handling. It will be updated against the actual app and its services before publication. It does not state that an unfinished app already collects, or does not collect, these data.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

JN86VI · jn86apps@outlook.com

Local data

Items, quantities, batches, locations, stock movements and rates would remain locally. Imports, exports and offline backup are explicit user operations. Scan images and extracted details are intended for on-device processing.

Permissions

Manual inventory needs no dangerous permission. Camera is requested only for scanning; selected images and import files use Android’s picker. Recognition is confirmed by you.

Advertising and purchases

The plan keeps every core feature free, with banner advertising and an optional one-time Google Play purchase to remove ads. There is no subscription. Actual local prices will come from Play; advertising providers, consent choices and SDK data handling must be specified before launch.

External services and sharing

Data you choose to export or share goes to the file location or app you select. Support and website links may open a browser; email opens your email app. GitHub Pages and any payment or advertising provider apply their own privacy rules. No complete provider list or automatic deletion by every provider is promised.

Retention and deletion

The plan keeps local records until you remove them or clear Android app data. Exported files and system backups are separate copies you manage separately. Local deletion does not erase a Play purchase or request a refund. Provider retention follows its own rules; no uniform retention period or complete automatic erasure is promised.

Your choices and contact

You can control permissions in Android and use the app’s deletion controls when available. For access, correction or deletion requests about information held by JN86VI, contact jn86apps@outlook.com. Sending email shares your address and message with us; we use them to answer your request and keep them only as needed for the inquiry or legal obligations. Do not send passwords or purchase tokens.
