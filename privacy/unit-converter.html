Unit Converter Privacy Policy

For the current development version of Unit Converter for Android (hu.jn86.unitconverter). Developer: JN86VI.

Offline operation and entered values

Conversions and calculations run on your device without a JN86 account or server. Entered numbers, selections and results are processed locally. Uncommitted editing drafts remain in memory; calculated results are recomputed rather than stored as authoritative results. The app does not send conversion inputs or results to JN86.

Settings, Favorites, Recent and History

App-private storage remembers language, theme and number-format settings, favorite feature IDs and ordering, up to 10 recent features and up to 50 History entries. History stores valid inputs, selections, feature and dataset references, ordering and time so a calculation can be reopened and recomputed. Favorites remain until removal or reset; Recent and History replace older entries at their limits. You can remove saved items, clear History or reset the relevant data. Resetting preferences affects settings separately.

Current input and Clear

The latest valid input and selections for each feature are stored separately on this device. A new valid input replaces them. Clear removes the saved input and remembers that the feature is empty, so an older value does not reappear on reopening. Raw drafts and calculated outputs are not saved in this store. Current input and rejected or temporary recovery data are excluded from Android backup.

Android system backup

If Android backup is enabled, settings and committed Favorites, Recent and History can be included in Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer and later restored. Current input, temporary files, diagnostics and Premium entitlement records or keys are excluded. JN86 provides no separate cloud backup or manual import/export. The Android backup provider controls transport, account and retention. Clearing local data does not necessarily delete an earlier backup; manage copies in device and account settings.

Permissions and external connections

The current app does not have Internet or network-state permissions and requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts or general shared-storage access. An internal Android signature permission supports library components; it is not a request for access to your personal data. An external browser and Android system backup can connect independently of the app.

Local diagnostics

Development diagnostics use local Android logs and fixed action messages, without conversion inputs, purchase receipts or consent payloads. There is no analytics, network crash-reporting or log-upload service. Android controls log and performance-profile storage and retention; JN86 does not specify a fixed retention period for those platform buffers.

External website, contact and support

Privacy and Support links open the JN86 website in a browser; Contact opens your email app. The site uses GitHub Pages, whose operator may process technical visit data. Optional Support development grants no digital content or advantage. A PayPal contribution is handled by PayPal, which may give the creator transaction and contact details. A Bitcoin transfer is visible on the public blockchain; a wallet address alone does not necessarily identify its sender to JN86. If you email us, your email address and message reach JN86VI. These external actions are initiated by you.

Retention and deletion

Local settings remain until reset, app-data clearing or uninstall; Android backup copies follow the backup provider rules. We keep contact messages only while the inquiry and necessary follow-up remain open, then delete or anonymize them unless a legal obligation or claim requires longer. Transaction information received by the creator is retained only for applicable tax or accounting duties and legal claims, then deleted or anonymized where possible. PayPal applies its own retention rules; JN86 cannot erase public blockchain records. We process contact messages to respond to requests on the basis of legitimate interests; legally required transaction records are processed to meet legal obligations.

Security

Settings and saved inputs use Android app-private storage. The app does not transmit conversion data to a JN86 server. Website links use HTTPS. Email, Android backup and payment providers apply their own security practices. Access to contact and transaction information received directly by JN86VI is limited to the creator and any parties needed for legal or accounting duties.

Advertising and Premium before release

The approved future model is banner advertising and an optional one-time Google Play Premium purchase that removes advertising only; conversion and calculation features stay free and offline. Advertising, Google Play Billing and a consent provider are not active in the current build, and no production ad requests or purchase data flow occur. Before these services are enabled, this policy will identify their actual providers, data, recipients, purposes, retention and required choices. Any future verified Premium cache must remain separate from settings and excluded from system backup. Voluntary Support never grants Premium or ad-free access.

Your choices and requests

You can reset settings or clear app data on your device and manage Android backups through your device or account. There is no JN86 account to delete. To request access, correction or deletion of information held by JN86VI, email us. Legally required records may have to be retained; public blockchain entries cannot be removed by JN86VI. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and complain to your data protection authority.
