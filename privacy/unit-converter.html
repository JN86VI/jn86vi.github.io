Unit Converter Privacy Policy

Unit Converter for Android (hu.jn86.unitconverter) is provided by JN86VI for adults aged 18 and over. After choosing the app language, you can convert units and calculate offline from the first launch. Advertising choices and purchases are not required to use these functions.

Offline operation and entered values

Conversions and calculations run on your device without a JN86 account or server. Entered numbers, selections and results are processed locally. Uncommitted editing drafts remain in memory; calculated results are recomputed rather than stored as authoritative results. The app does not send conversion inputs or results to JN86.

Settings, Favorites, Recent and History

App-private storage remembers language, theme and number-format settings, favorite feature IDs and ordering, up to 10 recent features and up to 50 History entries. History stores valid inputs, selections, feature and dataset references, ordering and time so a calculation can be reopened and recomputed. Favorites remain until removal or reset; Recent and History replace older entries at their limits. You can remove saved items, clear History or reset the relevant data. Resetting preferences affects settings separately.

Current input and Clear

The latest valid input and selections for each feature are stored separately on this device. A new valid input replaces them. Clear removes the saved input and remembers that the feature is empty, so an older value does not reappear on reopening. Raw drafts and calculated outputs are not saved in this store. Current input and rejected or temporary recovery data are excluded from Android backup.

Android system backup

If Android backup is enabled, settings and committed Favorites, Recent and History can be included in Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer and later restored. Current input, temporary files, diagnostics and Premium entitlement records or keys are excluded. JN86 provides no separate cloud backup or manual import/export. The Android backup provider controls transport, account and retention. Clearing local data does not necessarily delete an earlier backup; manage copies in device and account settings.

Permissions and external connections

The app declares internet access, network status and Play Billing permissions. Its SDKs can access the advertising ID (AD_ID) and basic phone state such as mobile network type (READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE). Android also allows scheduled SDK work and foreground services. Conversions require no GPS, camera, microphone or contacts. The browser, email and Android backup can connect separately.

Local diagnostics

App-owned diagnostics use local Android logs and fixed messages without conversion inputs, purchase tokens or consent payloads. UC has no own network analytics or crash-upload backend. Google UMP reporting, advertising performance and SDK crash diagnostics, and Billing technical telemetry are separate Google-service data flows. Android and providers control their own log retention.

External website, contact and support

Privacy and Support links open the JN86 website in a browser; Contact opens your email app. The site uses GitHub Pages, whose operator may process technical visit data. Optional Support development grants no digital content or advantage. A PayPal contribution is handled by PayPal, which may give the creator transaction and contact details. A Bitcoin transfer is visible on the public blockchain; a wallet address alone does not necessarily identify its sender to JN86. If you email us, your email address and message reach JN86VI. These external actions are initiated by you.

Retention and deletion

Local settings remain until reset, app-data clearing or uninstall; Android backup copies follow the backup provider rules. We keep contact messages only while the inquiry and necessary follow-up remain open, then delete or anonymize them unless a legal obligation or claim requires longer. Transaction information received by the creator is retained only for applicable tax or accounting duties and legal claims, then deleted or anonymized where possible. PayPal applies its own retention rules; JN86 cannot erase public blockchain records. We process contact messages to respond to requests on the basis of legitimate interests; legally required transaction records are processed to meet legal obligations. Local clearing or uninstall does not erase Google Play purchase records and is not a refund. Google and participating partners follow their own retention rules; no common deletion period or complete automatic provider erasure is promised.

Security

Settings and saved inputs use Android app-private storage. The app does not transmit conversion data to a JN86 server. Website links use HTTPS. Email, Android backup and payment providers apply their own security practices. Access to contact and transaction information received directly by JN86VI is limited to the creator and any parties needed for legal or accounting duties. Google SDK connections use HTTPS/TLS. Consent does not cause calculation inputs or saved lists to be uploaded to JN86.

Your advertising choices

After you choose the app language, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks your privacy status when the app is in the foreground and has an internet connection. To decide whether to show a form, UMP can contact Google before you agree to ads. It can send your IP address, app and device details, screen information, language and stored consent signals. UMP stores your choices on the device. Google can also process your interactions with the form.

Separate European and US privacy messages are configured in AdMob. UMP determines which message and choices apply; neither form is shown in every country. When UMP requires a privacy entry point, open Settings → Privacy settings in the app to review or change your choices, including withdrawing consent or opting out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising where available. The form and available options depend on your region. Your choices do not restrict the app’s calculation functions.

AdMob banner ads

The free app starts Google’s Next-Gen Mobile Ads SDK for AdMob only when it may request a banner while online and in the foreground. Unresolved or blocked advertising permission prevents requests. The applicable region and your actual privacy choices determine how advertising is handled, as described below.

Europe: EU/EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

UMP and European TCF signals determine the permitted ad mode. The app requests personalized ads only with the required consent. If you decline personalization, it requests non-personalized ads when their conditions are met, or eligible limited ads otherwise. Limited ads are not personalized; technical data remains necessary for delivery and fraud prevention, including fraud-prevention local storage when enabled. Your privacy choices are preserved. Declining personalization does not activate Premium ad removal.

Applicable US states

UMP records the applicable US privacy choices using Global Privacy Platform (GPP) signals. A supported opt-out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising causes Google to apply restricted data processing (RDP) to permitted ad requests. Google may still serve contextual, non-personalized ads. Opting out does not automatically remove advertising or grant Premium. An unresolved, invalid or unsupported applicable privacy signal prevents ad requests.

The AdMob US partner setting is “Only Google” for traffic from applicable US states. It covers requests both with and without RDP; these banner placements have no separately added US mediation sources. This setting is regional: it does not mean that only Google can supply ads worldwide. Elsewhere, participating ad partners depend on the applicable regional rules and settings.

Other countries

Outside the regions where European TCF or US GPP signals apply, the app does not automatically require a European consent signal. Ad requests depend on UMP’s current permission; Google’s SDK handles the applicable privacy signals and local requirements. The absence of a European or US form does not mean that local privacy rules do not apply, and is not treated by the app as consent to personalized advertising.

Depending on the region, your privacy choices and the ad mode, the ads SDK may collect and share data with Google and participating ad partners for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. This includes your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions such as launches and taps, performance and crash diagnostics such as startup time or reports of freezes and crashes, and identifiers. These can include the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device. Where a partner list is shown, it identifies potential providers, not the recipients of every request. RDP restricts the use of certain identifiers and data. The app sends no calculation inputs, results, history or JN86 user ID to AdMob.

The free app can show a built-in link to the JN86 website while no AdMob banner is available, including offline. Displaying this local promotion does not itself send an ad request. Opening the website in your browser requires an internet connection.

With verified Premium ad removal, the app shows no Google banner, local JN86 promotion or empty advertising strip, including offline. Premium does not necessarily stop UMP privacy checks, Play Billing or external services that you open yourself. Buying or restoring Premium is independent of your advertising choices.

Buying or restoring ad removal

Google Play Billing offers a one-time purchase to remove ads, with no subscription. All calculations remain free. Play shows the current regional price and handles payment; buying and restoring require an internet connection. To verify, acknowledge or restore a purchase, the app uses the product identifier, purchase state, purchase token and purchase data signed by Google Play. It checks the signature on the device without sending the token to a JN86 server. JN86 receives no payment card or bank account details. Billing can send technical diagnostics to Google even if you never open the purchase screen.

A protected record on the device keeps the last verified ad-free entitlement available offline. Restore checks current ownership with Google Play. A pending purchase does not grant ad removal. If Play confirms that the purchase is no longer owned, for example after revocation, the app removes the entitlement. Temporary connection problems do not erase the last verified entitlement. The local record is excluded from Android backup. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not erase the Play transaction or request a refund.

Your choices and requests

You can reset settings or clear app data on your device and manage Android backups through your device or account. There is no JN86 account to delete. To request access, correction or deletion of information held by JN86VI, email us. Legally required records may have to be retained; public blockchain entries cannot be removed by JN86VI. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests and complain to your data protection authority. Use the app’s privacy choices when available to review or withdraw consent. Android advertising-ID controls, Google data requests and a Play refund are different actions. We can help find provider controls but cannot guarantee deletion of all provider records. Do not send passwords, bank details or purchase tokens with a request.
