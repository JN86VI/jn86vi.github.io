Unit Converter

Privacy information

Pre-release privacy information for the Unit Converter Android app.

Core operation

Unit conversions and technical calculations that do not require external data are designed to run locally on the device. No user account is required for the core functionality.

Data stored on the device

Values entered for conversion are processed locally. App preferences and convenience data such as favorites or recent items may be stored locally on the device so the app can remember the user's choices.

Personal data

The core conversion functionality does not require a name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Before public release

Unit Converter is still in development. The final published build may include optional common services such as advertising or Google Play billing. If enabled, the exact services, data handling and links to their privacy information will be documented here before publication. This page will be updated to match the released app.
